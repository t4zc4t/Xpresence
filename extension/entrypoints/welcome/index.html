Xpresence

Shows what you're watching on your Discord profile, and only what you allow.

  1. Get the helper app

    Browsers can't talk to Discord directly, so a tiny helper does it. It only runs while something plays and needs no installer, just open it once.

  2. Open Discord

    Use the Discord desktop app, the web version can't show activities from other apps.

  3. Choose your sites

    Nothing is shared until you switch a site on. The eye on a tile hides titles for that site.

You're all set. Play something and look at your Discord profile.
